## Important Notice on Contribution Architecture

This Copyright Assignment Agreement ("CAA") represents the **preferred approach** for external code contributions to NyxWin:

1. **Unified Ownership:** A CAA transfers copyright ownership in accepted Contributions to the Project Owner, unifying copyright title and preserving the Project Owner's authority to manage licensing and commercial permissions as a single entity.
2. **Personal Non-Commercial License-Back:** In exchange, the Contributor receives a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, personal, non-transferable, and non-sublicensable license to use, modify, and distribute their own contribution for personal, research, educational, and non-commercial purposes.
3. **Contrast with Contributor License Agreement (CLA):** Unlike a CAA, a standard CLA grants only a non-exclusive license to the project while leaving copyright ownership fragmented among individual contributors. A CLA does **not** make the Project Owner the sole copyright owner.

### 1. Purpose and Identification of Contribution

The Project Owner is the author and maintainer of **NyxWin** ("the Project"). The Project Owner seeks to maintain unified copyright ownership and single-entity commercial licensing authority over the Project.

This Agreement applies exclusively to specific, identified contributions submitted by You to the Project Owner:
* **Identification:** Each Contribution covered by this Agreement must be clearly identified by pull request number, commit hash, file manifest, or written submission schedule agreed between the parties.
* **Consideration:** In consideration of the Project Owner reviewing, potentially accepting, and incorporating Your Contribution into the Project, and other good and valuable consideration (the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged), You agree to assign transferable copyright in Your accepted Contribution to the Project Owner subject to the terms herein.

### 2. Definitions

* **"Contribution"** means the specific original work of authorship (including code, patches, tests, documentation, or designs) intentionally submitted by You to the Project Owner for inclusion in the NyxWin repository.
* **"Assigned Rights"** means all transferable worldwide copyright, title, and interest in and to the accepted Contribution, including reproduction, adaptation, distribution, display, and sublicensing rights, and the right to sue for past, present, or future infringement.
* **"Effective Date"** means the date upon which the Project Owner explicitly accepts and merges the Contribution in writing or via authorized repository action.

### 3. Assignment of Copyright and Acceptance Conditions

1. **Conditional Assignment:** Subject to Section 3.2, You hereby unconditionally and irrevocably assign, transfer, and convey to the Project Owner all right, title, and interest in and to the Contribution, including all Assigned Rights, for the full term of copyright protection worldwide.
2. **Effective Date of Assignment:** The assignment of Assigned Rights becomes legally effective **solely and strictly upon the Effective Date** (explicit written acceptance and merge of the identified Contribution into the Project by the Project Owner).
3. **No Assignment of Unaccepted or Rejected Contributions:** If a proposed contribution is not explicitly accepted, is rejected, or is closed without merge by the Project Owner, **no assignment occurs**, no rights transfer to the Project Owner, and all copyright, title, and ownership in that unaccepted material remain exclusively with You.

### 4. Exclusion of Third-Party and Employer-Owned Material

1. **Originality Requirement:** You represent that the Contribution is entirely Your own original creation.
2. **Third-Party Code Exclusion:** You shall NOT include any third-party code, open-source libraries, or pre-existing materials in the assigned Contribution unless such third-party components are explicitly identified in writing, accompanied by their respective licenses, and separately accepted by the Project Owner as third-party dependencies rather than assigned works.
3. **Employer Intellectual Property Exclusion:** You shall NOT submit any material owned by Your employer or any third party under employment, consulting, or invention-assignment agreements, unless You have obtained and provided to the Project Owner prior explicit written authorization or an intellectual property disclaimer executed by an authorized representative of that employer or entity.

### 5. Scoped Non-Commercial License-Back and Downstream Permissions

Conditioned upon acceptance and merge of Your Contribution, the Project Owner hereby grants back to You a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, fully paid-up license to:
1. **Personal and Non-Commercial Exercise:** Use, reproduce, modify, adapt, publicly display, and perform Your Contribution; and
2. **Non-Commercial Distribution:** Distribute copies of Your Contribution (in original or modified form) solely for personal study, educational, academic research, hobbyist, or other permitted non-commercial community purposes.

*Downstream Permissions and Sublicensing Boundaries:*
* **Direct Non-Commercial License to Downstream Recipients:** Because this license-back is personal to You and does NOT grant You authority to create independent sublicenses, downstream recipients do not receive a sublicense from You. Instead, recipients of copies distributed by You receive a direct non-commercial license from the Project Owner under the terms of the Project's community license (the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 or its official successors), provided You include the Project's plain-text Required Notice and license terms with any such distribution.
* **Commercial Restrictions:** This license-back does NOT grant You the right to transfer this license, grant commercial sublicenses, or authorize third parties to use, distribute, or exploit Your Contribution for commercial advantage or monetary compensation. All commercial licensing and sublicensing rights remain exclusively with the Project Owner.

### 6. Contributor Attribution

The Project Owner agrees to maintain standard developer attribution in the public source-control commit history (author name and Git commit metadata) and documentation for all accepted Contributions.

### 7. Moral Rights and Non-Assignable Rights

1. **Non-Assignable Rights:** The parties recognize that in certain jurisdictions, moral rights (such as the right of attribution and integrity) cannot be assigned by law.
2. **Covenant of Non-Assertion:** To the maximum extent permitted under applicable law, You irrevocably agree not to assert any moral rights or rights against derogatory treatment against the Project Owner, its successors, assigns, licensees, or authorized users.

## Clauses Requiring Qualified Legal Review

Before this agreement can be approved for execution, qualified legal counsel must review and adapt:

1. **Conditional Assignment Mechanics:** Enforceability of conditional copyright assignment that takes effect strictly upon written acceptance/merge, and whether formal countersignature is required per contribution or per batch.
2. **Moral Rights Waivers Across Jurisdictions:** Validity and enforceability of moral rights non-assertion covenants across international civil law jurisdictions (e.g., France, Germany) where moral rights are statutory and non-waivable.
3. **Personal Non-Transferable License-Back:** Enforceability of a personal, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license-back under varying copyright regimes.
4. **Employer IP Disclaimers and Warranties:** Statutory sufficiency of individual employee warranties regarding employment agreements, and whether standardized employer waiver forms are necessary.
5. **Governing Law and Dispute Resolution:** Designating appropriate governing law, venue, and arbitration/litigation mechanisms once the formal contracting entity is established.
